01

Navigation & links

Two structural bugs that quietly sent visitors — and Google — to the wrong place.

B1

English menu was jumping to French pages (and vice-versa)

Live
Reported
Clicking Blog in the English menu landed on the French blog; French menu links dropped visitors onto English pages.
Cause
Three separate faults: a leftover migration redirect forcing /blog → /fr/blog, menu links that skipped the language prefix, and case-study cards that kept the old menu bar on screen after a click.
Fixed
Removed the bad redirect and language-locked every menu and card link. English stays English, French stays French.

SEO

20 old Google-indexed links were dead-ending on 404s

Live
Found
The site carries 109 redirects from the old URLs. Testing every one end-to-end, 20 landed on a 404 — all English legacy links (/en/…) pointed at the English route but with a leftover French slug that no longer exists.
Fixed
